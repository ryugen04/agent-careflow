## Situation

Codex now has an explicit `codex-careflow` entrypoint, but Claude relies on hooks and skills without an equally visible careflow launcher. The user's original failure mode includes agents not recognizing or remembering careflow mid-session.

## Background

Business machines use Claude for research/planning and review. A managed launcher gives the operator an explicit careflow-first Claude command while preserving the normal `claude` command.

## Assessment

A `claude-careflow` launcher cannot fix local Claude authentication, and it must not claim to produce reviews. It can, however, refresh durable `.careflow/INDEX.md` and `.agent/` before launching Claude, ensuring the session starts from current visible artifacts.

## Recommendation

Install `~/.local/bin/claude-careflow` through dotfiles, audit it, document it, and keep business review gates strict.
