## Situation

Business objective audit now fails only because the Claude review artifact is a placeholder. That is correct, but the workflow still needs a durable, repeatable Claude review request/execution path so the next Claude-capable session cannot miss the artifact contract.

## Background

The user's business workflow uses Claude for research/planning and Claude+Codex for review. The broader agent-careflow design requires handoff headers and order/result paths to be explicit at the top of every cross-agent request.

## Assessment

The right next step is not to fake the review. It is to make the review request/run path first-class, strict, and evidence-backed, then leave business audit failing until Claude actually produces a non-placeholder review.

## Recommendation

Implement or harden a Claude review request/execution command that emits the fixed header, points at PLAN/ORDER/SUBPLAN/RESULT, names the expected review artifact path, and instructs Claude to write a strict review with findings, evidence reviewed, and recommendation.
