## Situation

The system now has activation, handoff, dashboard, workspace, review, learning, install, and launcher behavior. However, completion of the user's broad objective is still judged through a compact objective-audit list, not through a requirement-by-requirement matrix that shows which original requirement each check proves.

## Background

The persistent goal requires preserving the full scope and not redefining success around existing passing tests. A traceability matrix makes the remaining gap explicit for Claude, Codex, and human operators across sessions.

## Assessment

Adding a matrix is aligned because it turns the broad "complete agent-careflow philosophy" objective into inspectable, durable evidence without weakening the strict business gate. It also helps future Claude-authenticated sessions know exactly what evidence is still missing.

## Recommendation

Implement an objective matrix/report backed by tests, save current output as ORD-018 evidence, and keep business completion blocked only by missing real Claude review evidence.
