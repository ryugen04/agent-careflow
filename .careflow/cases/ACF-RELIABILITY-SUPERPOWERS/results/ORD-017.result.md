## Summary

Added an explicit managed `claude-careflow` launcher through the dotfiles installer, matching the visible `codex-careflow` entrypoint without replacing or shadowing the real `claude` command. The launcher refreshes durable careflow context before starting Claude by running `agent-careflow dashboard --write` and `agent-careflow workspace --write`, then delegates with `exec claude "$@"`.

This strengthens Claude-to-Codex and Codex-to-Claude session continuity for business machines while preserving strict review gates: the launcher does not fabricate review evidence and does not fix local Claude authentication.
